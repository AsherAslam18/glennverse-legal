GlennVerse Account Deletion

If you would like to delete your GlennVerse account and associated data, you can do so directly from within the mobile application.

Steps to Delete Your Account

  1. Open the GlennVerse app.
  2. Navigate to Profile.
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm your deletion request.

What Happens After Deletion

  • Your account will be permanently removed.
  • Your personal data stored in our systems will be deleted.
  • Access to purchased books may be removed after account deletion.

Data Retention

Certain transaction records related to purchases may be retained for legal or financial compliance purposes.
